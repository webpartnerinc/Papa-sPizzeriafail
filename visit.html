Our Story

A cozy joint with Sanpete heart

Papa's Pizzeria sits on the east side of State Street in Fairview, Utah — a cozy little pizza joint in the heart of Sanpete County, surrounded by the mountain views and small-town charm that make this valley special.

The idea has never changed: bake every pie fresh to order, keep the room warm and welcoming, and treat everyone who walks through the door like a regular — from local families and youth teams to travelers just passing through the valley.

And the neighbors have noticed: a 4.9-star rating across more than 300 Google reviews has made Papa's one of the best-loved pizza stops in central Utah.
